Our Story

North Star Bakery began in 2018 as a weekend farmers market stand with a single deck oven and a ten-year-old sourdough starter. Thanks to loyal neighbors and early-morning commuters, we opened our permanent brick-and-mortar storefront on Elm Street to provide a welcoming gathering spot for fresh bread and coffee.

Sourcing Philosophy

Great bread starts with healthy soil and honest ingredients. We partner directly with regional grain mills for organic heritage wheat, source pasture-raised eggs and dairy from family farms within 60 miles, and avoid artificial preservatives or dough conditioners in every recipe.
